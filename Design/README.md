# Artwork attribution

The birthday-screen illustrations, Nanit logo, number digits, swirls, ringed
baby placeholders, back arrow, camera controls, and share arrow are **Nanit
proprietary artwork** from the original take-home Figma file:

[Nanit Happy Birthday design](https://www.figma.com/design/YQObKTFziOt9xIR5ySXqIf/Dev-Home-assignment?node-id=1-201)

This artwork is reproduced **solely for this take-home assignment**. No broader
license to the artwork is granted or claimed, and it must not be repurposed or
published as a separate asset library.

## How it was integrated

- The three themed illustrations are the original PNG exports.
- The digits 0–9, left/right swirls, Nanit logo, ringed placeholders, and back
  arrow are the original grouped vector artwork, exported directly as PDF with
  full stroke bounds. Nothing was redrawn or traced.
- The three themed camera controls use the original circle, camera, and plus
  SVG parts, composed at the exact measured Figma positions, then converted to vector PDF.
- The share arrow is the original SVG, converted to a vector PDF that preserves
  its original path, bounds, and padding. It was not substituted with a system
  icon.

A system (SF) font substitutes for the proprietary Figma typeface; the
assignment explicitly permits this.

## Scope

This covers the **artwork only**. It is distinct from the application source
code, for which no separate license is granted here.
